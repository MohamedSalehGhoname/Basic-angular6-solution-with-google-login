## Data safety answers

- **Does the app collect or share user data?** Yes, it collects.
- **Personal info → Email address:** collected, required, for account
  identification (sign-in). Not shared, not used for advertising.
- **App activity / Files and docs:** the clipboard entries, password entries and
  files a user stores. Collected (stored on the server) but **encrypted end to
  end**; the publisher cannot read them. Not shared.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS), and the contents are also
  encrypted on the device before sending.
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes — in the app for individual items, and by
  email for the whole account.

## Content rating

Productivity, no user-generated public content, no ads, no purchases → Everyone.

## What the reviewer may ask about

- **The app reads the clipboard.** Only on desktop, only while the user turns
  "Capture copies" on. The Android app never reads the clipboard in the
  background; content arrives only when shared to it deliberately.
- **Test credentials.** Reviewers need to get past Google sign-in: hand them a
  throwaway Google account, or a short note that any Google account works and
  the vault passphrase is set by the user on first run.
