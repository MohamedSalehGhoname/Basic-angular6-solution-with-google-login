GhoClipboard — Deleting your account

Last updated: 25 September 2026

You can delete your GhoClipboard account and everything stored for it yourself, from inside the app. It takes effect immediately and needs nobody's approval.

Deleting some of your data, without deleting the account

You do not have to delete the account to remove what is in it. In the app you can delete any single entry with the Delete button beside it, empty a whole collection with Clear all on the Clipboard or Secrets page, and delete password groups with everything filed in them. Each of these removes the entry from the server and from every one of your devices straight away. You can also set new clipboard entries to expire after an hour, a day or a week, and they are deleted when the time is up.

Deleting the account, from the app

  1. Open GhoClipboard on your phone, on your computer, or in the browser.
  2. Sign in and unlock your vault.
  3. Go to Settings and scroll to Delete account.
  4. Press Delete my account.
  5. Type delete in the box that appears and press Delete permanently.

Your other signed-in devices lock themselves and clear their copy as soon as they hear about it.

What is deleted

  • Your vault, which holds the only copy of the key your data is encrypted with.
  • Every clipboard entry: text, links and images.
  • Every password entry and every group you filed them in.
  • Every record of the files you sent.
  • The copy each of your devices keeps for working offline.

This cannot be undone. Because the vault holds the only copy of your key, nothing that was encrypted with it can be read again — by you, by us, or by anyone holding a copy of the database. Export anything you want to keep before you start.

What remains, and for how long

  • The files themselves. Files you sent are held with GTDrive (drive.ghonameservices.com) in append-only storage that does not accept deletions, and are erased automatically when the retention period set for the service ends. Their keys are deleted with your vault, so from the moment you delete your account nobody can read them — encrypted files are unreadable ciphertext, and files you chose to send unencrypted are unreachable because the record naming them is gone.
  • Ordinary web server logs (IP address and time of request), kept briefly for running and securing the service. They contain no clipboard content.
  • Your Google account is not touched: signing in used it for identification only. You may also remove GhoClipboard's access from your Google account permissions.

If you cannot reach the app

Write to info@ghomicrosystems.com from the email address you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted, and it will be removed from the server.
