GhoClipboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

GhoClipboard keeps a clipboard, passwords and files in step across your devices. It is built so that the people running the service cannot read what you store in it.

What the app stores for you

  • Clipboard entries (text and images) that you add or that the desktop app captures.
  • Password entries and the groups you file them in.
  • Files you send to the clipboard.

All of it is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your passphrase before it is sent anywhere. The passphrase never leaves your device and is never transmitted. The server holds unreadable ciphertext; nobody operating the service, and nobody who obtained a copy of the database, can decrypt it. Losing the passphrase and the recovery code means the data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

What the service can see

  • Your account identifier and email address, from signing in with Google. This identifies which encrypted items belong to you.
  • Technical facts needed to run the service: the size and timing of items, the number of files, and ordinary web server logs (IP address, time of request).

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind, and we profile nobody.

Files

Files you send are stored with GTDrive (drive.ghonameservices.com), operated by the same publisher on Cloudflare R2. With file encryption on — the default — the file is encrypted on your device first and stored under a random name, so the storage holds only ciphertext. If you turn encryption off, files are stored as they are, under their own name. Stored files are deleted automatically after the retention period set for the service.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Internet — to sync with the server.
  • Notifications — to show transfer progress and completed downloads.
  • Biometrics — optional; unlocks your vault with your fingerprint. The fingerprint stays in the phone's secure hardware and is never sent anywhere.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older) — to save downloads into your Downloads folder. Newer versions need no permission for this.

The app reads the clipboard only on desktop computers, and only while you have "Capture copies" switched on. Android does not permit background clipboard access and the app does not attempt it: on a phone, content arrives only when you share it to the app deliberately.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It passes through the infrastructure needed to deliver the service: the hosting provider, Cloudflare (which carries the traffic), Google (sign-in only), and the file storage described above.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item, or clear a whole collection, from inside the app. To remove your account and everything stored for it, write to the address below and it will be deleted from the server.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be announced in the app.
